# Editing the website (staff guide)

Go to **www.donmacltd.com/admin** and sign in with your GitHub account.

## Collections

| Collection | What you can change |
|---|---|
| Site settings > Contact details and homepage | Phone, emails, address, office hours, homepage headline, hero images, key statistics |
| Site settings > Partners and brands | Authorised partnerships and the brands supplied and integrated |
| Site settings > Company | Purpose, vision, mission, values, timeline, Why Donmac, compliance register, client references |
| Site settings > Industries | Industry challenges, solutions and links |
| Business divisions | Hero, introduction, services (with images), strengths, featured projects, call to action, SEO text |
| Projects | Add or edit case studies, specs, outcomes and galleries |
| Vehicle catalogue | Add vehicles with specifications, photos and brochures |
| Leadership | Profiles, approved photos and messages |
| News and insights | Articles with category, date, author, image and body |

## Publishing workflow

1. Edit or create an entry and press **Save**. It is saved as a **Draft** and is not public.
2. Move it to **In review** so a manager can check it (use the preview pane on the right).
3. When approved, set it to **Ready** and press **Publish**. The site rebuilds and the change is live in about two minutes.

## Content rules

* Publish only verified facts: no unconfirmed figures, clients, certifications, authorisations, prices, stock or charging stations.
* Only list a brand under **Authorised partnerships** when Donmac holds a current certificate.
* Every image needs alt text describing what it shows.
* Upload photos at least 1600 px wide (JPEG, under 1 MB if possible).
* Do not use em-dashes; follow the Donmac house style.
* Vehicle specifications must come from the manufacturer's official data sheet, with the range test cycle stated (e.g. WLTP or CLTC).
