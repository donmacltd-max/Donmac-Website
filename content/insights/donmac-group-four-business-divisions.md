Donmac was incorporated in Lusaka on 23 May 2011 as a Zambian citizen-owned supplier focused on quality and client-centred service. In 2026 the company consolidated into **Donmac Group**, organised around four principal business divisions:

- **Donmac Energy**: solar EPC, battery storage, hybrid back-up, solar water pumping, rural electrification and O&M.
- **Donmac ICT**: enterprise hardware, networking, data centres, cybersecurity, cloud, digital identity and surveillance.
- **Donmac e-Mobility**: electric vehicles, AC and DC charging infrastructure and fleet electrification.
- **Donmac Specialized Sourcing**: international procurement of industrial, mining, energy and ICT equipment, with freight and customs coordination.

## One group, one accountable partner

Each division has dedicated technical expertise, but all four share a common backbone: engineering and technical services, procurement and supply chain, project management and QA/QC, HSE and compliance, and finance and governance. That structure lets the Group take on single-discipline contracts and complex, multi-technology programmes with the same confidence.

## What it means for clients

Clients gain a single point of accountability for work that crosses disciplines, such as a mine site that needs hybrid solar, site ICT and electric utility vehicles, or a public institution that needs equipment sourced, installed and supported.
