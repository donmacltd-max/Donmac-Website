Electric vehicles are now available in the categories most Zambian fleets rely on, from 4WD pickups to buses and motorcycles. The organisations that benefit most treat electrification as a structured programme. These are the six steps we follow with fleet clients.

## 1. Fleet energy assessment

Collect real data on each vehicle: daily distance, routes, duty cycle, dwell times and current fuel and maintenance spend.

## 2. Vehicle suitability study

Match each duty cycle to available electric models, checking range, payload, terrain and towing needs.

## 3. Route and range analysis

Test the longest and hardest routes against realistic range, with margin for load, weather and battery ageing.

## 4. Charging infrastructure planning

Decide where vehicles will charge: at the depot overnight, at destinations or on route. Size chargers to schedules and check site electrical capacity. Solar and storage can reduce grid dependence.

## 5. Total cost of ownership modelling

Compare electric and internal-combustion options over the full life of the vehicle, including energy, maintenance, charging infrastructure and residual value.

## 6. Operational transition planning

Phase the change, train drivers and technicians, and monitor performance so lessons from the first vehicles shape the next.
