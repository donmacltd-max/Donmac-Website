Mining operations and project sites often run offices, camps, workshops and control rooms far from a strong grid connection. Diesel fills the gap, but it brings fuel logistics, maintenance and price risk. A well-engineered hybrid system, combining solar PV, lithium battery storage and the existing generator or grid, can carry much of that load.

## Start with the load

The most common cause of an under-performing system is an assumed load. Before sizing anything we measure or reconstruct the site's daily load profile: what runs, when, and how much it draws at peak. That profile drives every later decision.

## Size storage for the job it has to do

Batteries can serve different purposes: shifting daytime solar into the evening, riding through outages, or reducing generator run-hours. Each leads to a different capacity and inverter configuration. Lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4) chemistry is the usual choice for its cycle life and thermal stability.

## Integrate, protect and monitor

The hybrid inverter is where solar, batteries, grid and generator meet. Correct protection coordination, earthing and surge protection keep the system safe; remote monitoring makes faults visible before they become outages.

## Plan for maintenance from day one

Panels need cleaning, connections need checking and firmware needs updating. A maintenance plan agreed at handover protects the yield the system was designed for.
